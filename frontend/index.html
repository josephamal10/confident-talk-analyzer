How it works

  1. Pick a situationInterview, JAM, presentation, news reading and more: each has its own rules and timer.
  2. Record yourselfSpeak into your microphone. Topics, questions or a teleprompter appear on screen.
  3. Get analysedSpeech models measure pace, fillers, pauses, pitch variation and how confident your voice sounds.
  4. Get coachedAn AI coach reviews your content, checks your structure and writes a stronger version.
  5. Track progressReplay old sessions, see your weakest skills and get told what to practise next.

What it measures

Pace (words per minute) Filler words Hesitation pauses Pitch variation Vocal confidence Hedging language Reading accuracy Timing Structure (STAR, PREP...) Topic relevance Slide coverage
