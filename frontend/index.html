How it works

From your voice to a coaching plan

  1. Pick a situationInterview, JAM, presentation, news reading and more: each has its own rules and timer.
  2. Record yourselfSpeak into your microphone. Topics, questions or a teleprompter appear on screen.
  3. Get analysedSpeech models measure pace, fillers, pauses, pitch variation and how confident your voice sounds.
  4. Get coachedAn AI coach reviews your content, checks your structure and writes a stronger version.
  5. Track progressReplay old sessions, see your weakest skills and get told what to practise next.

Fillers, hedges and pauses

Hear what your listeners hear

Every "um", "uh" and "you know" is marked in your transcript, along with hedges like "I think" and "maybe" that make you sound less sure. Pauses are timed from the audio itself, so you can tell a confident pause from a hesitation.

AI coach

A coach that improves your answer, not replaces it

The coach checks your answer against the right framework (STAR for interviews, PREP for opinions, problem-solution-ask for pitches), quotes what you actually said, and rewrites it using only your own facts. It never invents numbers or stories for you.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. Every practice mode, the analysis and the AI coach are free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. Click "Try it as a guest" to start straight away. Create an account if you want to keep your progress for longer than 7 days.

Which languages does it support?

English, including Indian-accented English, which it is tested on. The coach and scores are written for English speaking practice.

What do I need to use it?

A browser and a microphone. A laptop or phone mic works fine; a quiet room gives the most accurate results.

How long does an analysis take?

Roughly as long as your recording. You can watch each stage, from transcription to tone, as it runs.
